# Arduino LED Projects

Beginner Arduino projects focused on digital output control and basic embedded programming.

## Projects

### 1. LED Blink
A simple Arduino project that blinks an LED at 1-second intervals.

**File:** `LED_Blink.ino`

**Concepts:** `pinMode()`, `digitalWrite()`, `delay()`

### 2. Three LED Blink
Controls three LEDs connected to Arduino digital pins 8, 9, and 10. The LEDs blink sequentially.

**File:** `3_LED_Blink.ino`

**Pins:** Red – 8 | Yellow – 9 | Green – 10

**Concepts:** Digital GPIO, multiple outputs, sequential control
